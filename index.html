About Golden Transport Service

M/S GOLDEN TRANSPORT SERVICE was established in 1998 by Aslambhai Saiyed in Surat, Gujarat. Being in the transportation business, starting as a small broker in the year 1986 and establishing a well-reputed Lorry brokering firm, M/S LUCKY TRANSPORT, Aslambhai Saiyed has built a strong and reliable network of highly professional and experienced lorry fleets. Exhibiting trust and reliance by our credible and esteemed clients, we started Golden Transport Service, marking our entry into booking and delivery service in the transportation industry. Since then, we haven't looked back, and today we have been providing our services to more than 5000 customers at 50+ locations spread across 4 states, i.e., Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu, expanding our client base significantly. We strive to provide economical, competitive, and reliable transportation services to our valuable customers.

  • Experienced and professional team
